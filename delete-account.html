GDPR & Account Privacy

Delete Your SheliApp Account

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. This page explains what gets deleted, what is retained, and how to submit your request.

What happens when you delete your account

Deleting your SheliApp account is permanent and cannot be undone. We will process your request within 30 days in accordance with GDPR.

Data type Action Retention period
Profile & login credentials Deleted Immediately upon confirmation
Virtual pets & pet data Deleted Immediately upon confirmation
In-game items & inventory Deleted Immediately upon confirmation
Quest progress & activity history Deleted Immediately upon confirmation
Friends list & social connections Deleted Immediately upon confirmation
SheliCoin transaction logs Retained Up to 7 years (legal / financial compliance obligation)
Anonymised aggregated analytics Retained Indefinitely (no personal identifiers)

How to request account deletion

Choose either method below. Both result in the same permanent deletion of your account.

Option 1 — Inside the app

  1. Open SheliApp on your device and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
  3. Go to Settings → Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account and follow the confirmation prompt.

Option 2 — Request via email form below

  1. Fill in the form on this page with the email address linked to your account.
  2. We will send a confirmation link to that email address within a few minutes.
  3. Click the link in the email to confirm and permanently delete your account.
